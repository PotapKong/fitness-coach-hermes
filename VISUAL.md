# Visual report contract

A chart is a data artifact, not a diagnosis or a moral grade. The agent may use its image-generation tool for an editorial presentation of **synthetic** or owner-authorized private records, but must not trust generated numbers, labels, scales, or exercise claims without comparing every visible datum to `coach.py trends` and `report` output. Never upload a health record to an image provider without explicit informed consent for that external processing. If the user has consented only to local logs, use the verified local PNG renderer instead.

## Status and color semantics

- **Slate/gray — unknown:** missing meals, unconfirmed completeness, photo-estimated portions, unknown macros, no owner-approved goal, or incomplete nutrient coverage. Missing ≠ zero. Do not make the bar look like an explicit zero.
- **Teal — within an owner-approved range:** `weekly_range_status=within_user_range` only when all 7 food days were confirmed complete, an owner-approved weekly-average energy range was recorded, and no photo-estimated food was logged. This is agreement of **recorded energy** with a chosen plan, not proof of healthy eating. `food_complete` confirms the owner's report, not true completeness or nutrient accuracy.
- **Amber — review a difference from the owner's range:** `weekly_range_status=outside_user_range` under the same evidence gate. Never label it “bad,” “cheat,” “failed,” or “unhealthy.” A single day does not diagnose anything. When status is `null`, keep neutral, not red/amber.
- **Micronutrients:** display units, source and coverage only. No red/green deficiency grading from a food log, no fabricated DRI line. DRI/RDA/AI/UL differ and depend on the individual; even a complete log is not a lab test.
- **Activity:** distinguish “recorded workout” from “no record”; do not color a rest day negatively. Only compare completed vs planned activity when an owner-approved plan and complete workout log exist. Do not use calories burned as exact compensation for food.

## GPT Image workflow (optional)

1. Obtain explicit consent for sharing the selected **personal** report values with an external image provider; otherwise work entirely locally. Never send names, conditions, photos or exact medical details unless needed and explicitly authorized. Synthetic examples need no health-data consent.
2. Select the right packet: `coach.py snapshot --scope entry` just after a new entry, `--scope day` for an end-of-day view (includes Monday-to-today brief), `--scope week` for the ISO Monday–Sunday wrap-up. Run `report`/`trends` when needed for detailed verification. Freeze a short fact sheet of exact dates, units, numbers, missing entries, coverage and privacy-safe labels.
3. Provide the image model the fact sheet as hard constraints. Make an editorial mobile-first layout with purposeful color, typography, information hierarchy and clear unknown states; intensity of the palette follows the owner's style preference, not a health judgment. Never ask it to estimate or invent missing values or personalized norms.
4. Inspect the returned image visually and compare **each** visible date, number, unit, mark, legend **and depicted food/exercise object** against the fact sheet. Reject pseudo-text, altered data, invented ingredients, wrong day order, misleading bar lengths, clipping, or an unapproved traffic-light assessment. If any check fails, regenerate or fall back to an exact text report (or the legacy local PNG if accepted by the owner). Do not silently correct generated facts with code and call the result model-only.
5. Deliver as a native image to the explicitly specified chat only. A model-generated card is an *illustrated report* with a verification gate; it is not the canonical record. The local JSON journal and text report remain authoritative.

The model can make a more stylish single image, but it cannot guarantee exact charts. The CLI computes an optional **weekly recorded-energy range status** under strict coverage conditions; this is not a nutritional-quality score, a medical norm, or a safe clinical interpretation. Keep this limitation in the user-facing description.

## Three distinct jobs, not one repeated bar chart

- **On entry — compact receipt:** one confirmed meal/workout/weight and its source, today's recorded subtotal (if applicable), one short context line. Do not imply the day is complete or show a red goal shortfall while the day is ongoing. Deliver an image only if the person opted into image generation and private-data transfer; otherwise a compact text receipt is the safe default.
- **Day close — editorial day sheet:** logged food with measured-versus-estimated coverage, known macro and micronutrient amounts where available, activity recorded, then a small *week-to-date* strip labeled by logged days. Ask the user before `close-day --confirmed-by-user`; a later meal reopens that day.
- **Week close — distinct weekly spread:** ISO Monday–Sunday, recorded days/confirmed-complete days, average logged energy only **over those complete days**, activity minutes and training days as observations, and sparse weight points with dates. A blank day stays unknown. Do not compare generic workout minutes to WHO moderate/vigorous thresholds because the logger does not capture intensity; do not turn daily DRI values into a weekly allowance or infer deficiency from one week.

Avoid decorative gauges, continuous streaks, fake precision, and seven identical bars across every surface. Different layouts may share typography, materials, palette and accessibility contrast. Labels and numbers are facts; texture and composition are illustration. Read the report at Telegram thumbnail size before delivery.

## Art direction and prompt skeletons

The neutral default uses warm off-white (`#F6F7F4`), deep ink (`#14232B`), blue (`#135E7B`) for activity, muted teal (`#126B63`) for logged nutrition, violet (`#614B91`) for time-series context, and terracotta (`#9B3E35`) as a restrained accent, **never a punishment**. An owner-chosen bold style may instead use ink/charcoal, cobalt and vivid lime with tested contrast. Color always has a written status and a different mark/shape. Keep Russian labels short and the main fact visibly larger than decoration. These are design tokens, not computed health judgments. WCAG references and research links are in `EVIDENCE.md`.

For each scope, feed the **exact** current `snapshot` output through a minimal prompt. Replace brackets with verified values; never send a full raw journal to the image provider. If exact bar lengths matter, do not ask the model to draw them: send the canonical text/JSON or the local deterministic card instead.

- **Entry prompt:** “Square premium mobile receipt, style [chosen profile], one bold numeral. Copy exactly: [date], [latest logged item and confirmed portion/duration], [source/estimate], [recorded day subtotal if applicable]. Depict only the named new food or recorded exercise; if item is generic/unknown use an abstract non-food scene. No empty plates, invented ingredients, body, goals or progress ring. Neutral status while the day is open.”
- **Day prompt:** “Portrait day sheet for Telegram, style [chosen profile], one main recorded fact, a scene featuring [identified recorded foods on that date, if authorized], below it food coverage, known macros, activity and a quiet Monday-to-today line. Exact allowed text: [short immutable fact list]. Missing fields say ‘нет данных’; if day not owner-confirmed complete, do not show target percentage. Do not invent food groups, nutrients, calorie burn or medical conclusions.”
- **Week prompt:** “Square/portrait magazine spread, style [chosen profile], distinct nutrition and movement zones, one elegant sequence of date marks instead of seven identical bars. If showing foods use only [named foods selected from that week] with caption ‘часть записей’; if `food_scene=playful`, mark an oversized spread ‘образная подача’, never literal quantity. Exact allowed text: [ISO week start/end], [complete days / 7], [average recorded kcal on complete days or unknown], [observed activity minutes and days], [only actually recorded weight points]. No WHO completion badge without intensity and full activity history; no nutrient deficiency grading.”

Try one image-model draft, inspect every visible label/number/scale and contrast at phone size, then either one focused regeneration or send the exact local fallback. A beautiful wrong number is a failed report.

## Personalization and food-scene contract

`visual-profile --style bold-athletic|high-energy|editorial|minimal|adaptive --food-scene literal|playful|abstract --confirmed-by-user` stores **only the owner's chosen visual preferences** in the private journal; `snapshot` returns them along with `recorded_food_items` and `recorded_workout_items`. The profile does not store gender, body type, age, diagnoses, or image-generation consent. Ask separately before sending any real records to an external model; an existing style choice is **not** that consent. Without a chosen style, use a readable neutral default and offer options instead of inferring taste from gender or body measurements.

- `bold-athletic`: large modern Cyrillic sans, ink/charcoal with vivid cobalt and lime, sharp editorial crop, sporty materials. This is an **aesthetic choice**, not a men's preset. `high-energy`: warmer saturated palette and playful rhythm; `editorial`: warm paper and measured type; `minimal`: crisp data-forward layouts; `adaptive`: rotate among compatible compositions based on actual logged content, user preferences and last accepted card. Across successive cards change at least three of composition, focal object, viewpoint, material, lighting, typography placement and information architecture. Keep accessible contrast and written status; color cannot manufacture a health grade.
- **Depict only named, confirmed foods from `recorded_food_items`.** The snapshot gives exact meal labels, optional weighed grams, source and recorded kcal, not ingredients inferred from a generic name. A name like “lunch” or a calorie value does **not** authorize a particular dish. If the label lists several foods, illustrate only those. Product/plate photos supplied by the person may serve as reference only with permission and may not be presented as documentary evidence after generative alteration. For photos/estimates mark uncertainty; do not draw sauces, toppings, brands or a full serving when details are unknown.
- **No empty plate as a default symbol.** If no foods are identified, show a neutral abstract typographic/data scene, not an empty bowl, missing-food shaming or a fabricated meal. If one dish is identified, make it the main object with its exact label. If several are identified, use a spread, segmented tray, pantry shelf or collage; label pictured foods as **“часть записей”** when only a selection is shown. For a week with many entries, do not pretend that a single plate contains the entire week.
- A bucket, tub, mountain or scale is a **playful metaphor**, available only when `food_scene=playful` was explicitly chosen and some actual foods/portions are known. It must be labelled “образная подача” and never imply literal mass, a meal-sized serving, overeating or insufficient eating. **Calories do not determine volume.** If portions are unknown, there is no grounded “large/small” food pile; choose variety of recorded foods or a non-quantity metaphor instead. For low-energy or incomplete logs, avoid tiny/shaming portions and “empty” visuals. Do not use body size, sex, activity or weekly calories as an automatic trigger for vessel size.
- **Body and gender:** a recorded weight is not a body shape, a gender inference or an aesthetic instruction. Do not synthesize a body, face, before/after or “ideal physique” from measurements. If a person volunteered gender, training context or body data, use it only where relevant to requested wording or clinical referral; for visual character/likeness require a separate explicit request and consent to external processing. Ask which style they actually want; a masculine style may be chosen by anyone.
- Before sending, compare every depicted item with the selected source meal names and the **period**: on-entry only the new meal, day card only that day's known foods, weekly card only foods logged within that week. Reject an appealing image that quietly adds a salad, oil, snack or protein shake. Never claim that a food photo proves the nutrient amounts or the day's dietary quality.
